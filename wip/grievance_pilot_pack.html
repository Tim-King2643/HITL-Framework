2. The transition, part by part

GiGi’s scenario: AI takes the administrative shell — intake, precedent research, case preparation and documentation — while the HRBP Manager keeps interpretation, determination, escalation judgment and accountability. Stated in the six categories, the parts land differently against the Judgment ceiling. The proposed relationships are Claude’s reading of her scenario, for Tim and GiGi to confirm or correct in the session.

What the flags mean. Intake, case preparation and documentation have AI producing work, which is past Judgment; at Critical Consequence of Error the ceiling cannot be overridden. Readiness findings for those parts are findings about a transition the reference model does not currently allow. That is useful evidence, not a problem: if the parts behave differently in the pilot, it is the case for giving processes like 7.4.4 activities of their own — a decision to take once, for all 18 processes that have none, after the pilots.

Current state. GiGi’s draft describes it as human judgment with AI assistance limited or absent, which could be Human-only or Judgment. Settle it per part at the start of the session; with ABC Test Org, 7.4.4 can be assessed through the Assess panel first, so the pilot starts from a recorded assessment.

5. Tim’s draft answers to GiGi’s seven questions

From section 11 of her v0.1 draft. Drafts for Tim to edit before they are shared; nothing here is agreed until both have said so.

Answered in your review
1. Is GR-012 assessing human and organizational operating conditions, not re-testing the technology?

Yes, agreed. Trust / Evidence Quality takes the technical lane’s evidence as given and asks whether the person can see it, understand its limits and rely on it appropriately.

Deferred to the pilots
2. Which dimensions are true gates, and which can stay Yellow with a mitigation plan?

Agreed to keep the six as a hypothesis. Let HRBP Manager, Recruiter and CHRO tell us which really behave as gates; nothing needs deciding before this first pilot.

Proposed answer
3. Should a High / Critical consequence activity automatically use a weakest-link rule?

Yes. It matches the work side, where High and Critical ceilings cannot be overridden: at that level of consequence, one failed dimension should not be averaged away. 7.4.4 is Critical, so this pilot tests it directly.

Deferred to after the three pilots
4. Where should Human Agency sit?

As a working answer, in GR-013 as its own layer, with GR-012’s Decision authority and Psychological safety findings feeding it — which matches your architecture in section 6. We confirm after the pilots.

Proposed answer
5. What evidence should the sandbox store for each readiness finding?

Your assessment record, plus the fields the work side already uses: evidence type, source role, reference, confidence and date — so both sides record evidence the same way. For now that is the record’s design, used by hand in this worksheet; nothing goes into the sandbox until the logic has survived the pilots.

Proposed answer; not tested by this pilot
6. How should multiple Responsible roles be handled?

Separate findings per role, rolled up weakest-link where Consequence of Error is High or Critical (ties to question 3). 7.4.4 has only an Accountable role, so the Recruiter pilot is where this gets tested.

Partly answered in your review
7. What would count as GR-012 having “held without modification”?

Your test — the same questions hold across all three roles and produce different findings — plus two more: no dimension added, dropped or redefined between pilots, and every finding traceable to recorded evidence.

  • GR-012 acceptance wording — proposed, needs GiGi’s agreement. Change “scores all 10 readiness dimensions” to “produces a finding, with its evidence, for each of the ten dimensions”, matching her instrument. Not changed until she agrees.
  • GR-013 sequencing — recorded. Meaningful Human Control / Human Agency follows the three pilots, so it sharpens GR-012 rather than repeating it (her review’s sequence).
