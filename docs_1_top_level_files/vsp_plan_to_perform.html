Description

This value stream comprises one APQC PCF process group: 9.1 Perform Planning and Management Accounting — spanning 5 Level 3 processes and 23 Level 4 activities.

At the activity level, this value stream's work breaks down as 11 Transaction activities (rules-based processing suited to automation), 10 Knowledge activities (judgment, synthesis, and interpretation-driven work), and 2 Document activities (content generation). No Decision- or Exception-pattern activity exists within this value stream.

At M3 (HITL Design Intent), the value stream's aggregate human-involvement ratio is 51%, down from a 74% undesigned baseline — a 23-point AI convergence opportunity.

Design Narrative

Plan-to-Perform is one of two Strategy-tier value streams in PCF 9.0 rather than Finance's only one — 9.1.1 (Develop and manage financial resources strategy) is CFO's own strategy-formation L3, distinct from Tax Strategy-to-Compliance's separate CFO-owned apex (9.9.1), since both streams open with an Executive-scope strategy step even though Finance's role catalog concentrates domain strategy under a single CFO rather than splitting it across multiple C-suite peers the way IT does across CIO/CISO/CDO. Director of FP&A then holds Accountable for the stream's three core execution L3s — budgeting/forecasting (9.1.2), cost accounting and control (9.1.3), and cost management (9.1.4) — the clear majority of the stream's work. SVP Finance closes the stream with financial-performance evaluation (9.1.5), a review-and-assessment layer that sits above Director of FP&A's execution rather than beneath it. Its steepest AI-absorption opportunity sits in 9.1.2.1 (Develop and maintain budget policies and procedures, Document-pattern, -32 points).

Handoff Points

Plan-to-Perform opens with CFO-accountable strategy formation (9.1.1), then hands to Director of FP&A for the stream's core execution work — budgeting/forecasting, cost accounting, and cost management (9.1.2–9.1.4).

Director of FP&A's three L3s move budget policy through periodic budgets/forecasts to variance analysis and cost-driver management, the stream's largest single lane by both L3 and activity count.

SVP Finance closes the stream with financial-performance evaluation (9.1.5) — assessing customer/product profitability and cost-improvement outcomes — before its outputs (approved budget, forecast, performance scorecard) hand off to every other Finance value stream that consumes budget/forecast data downstream.

Governed Decision Points

No Decision- or Exception-pattern activity in this value stream — every step here is Knowledge, Document, Transaction, or Mixed without an embedded Decision/Exception component.
