⇓ Download as PDF

Description

This value stream comprises one APQC PCF process group: 7.6 Redeploy and Retire Employees — spanning 3 Level 3 processes and 6 Level 4 activities.

At the activity level, this value stream's work breaks down as 4 Transaction activities (rules-based processing suited to automation), 1 Decision activity (judgment-and-authority gates that stay human by design), and 1 Knowledge activity (judgment, synthesis, and interpretation-driven work). No Exception-pattern activity exists within this value stream.

At M3 (HITL Design Intent), the value stream's aggregate human-involvement ratio is 48%, down from a 70% undesigned baseline — a 22-point AI convergence opportunity.

Design Narrative

Move-to-Exit is the first value stream in this framework where a single role — HR Business Partner Manager — is Accountable for every L3 process, a confirmed structural finding rather than under-differentiation: published offboarding-process literature identifies diffused ownership as the primary failure mode for exit workflows, making concentrated single-role accountability the correct shape here. Human judgment stays most anchored in 7.6.1 (Manage Promotion and Demotion Process, Decision-pattern, the smallest Δ M1→M3 in the value stream at -12 points). This value stream also produced the framework's first confirmed specialist-role gap: 7.6.3's Accountable assignment is explicitly partial, since International HR Management is a recognized specialist discipline the current role catalog does not name — distinct in kind from Reward-to-Retain's HR/Finance boundary, which was a split between two existing roles rather than the absence of one.

Handoff Points

Move-to-Exit is the framework's first value stream with a single Accountable role: HR Business Partner Manager is Accountable for all three L3s and every step in this process flow, consistent with the offboarding-literature finding that diffused ownership is the primary failure mode for exit processes.

The real handoffs in this value stream sit outside HR rather than between HR roles. 7.6.2 Manage Separation triggers coordinated workstreams with Payroll, IT/Security, and Facilities against a shared offboarding checklist — final pay, access removal, asset return — outside HR's accountability scope, and escalates to Legal only for contested or high-risk terminations, not as a routine participant.

7.6.3 Relocate Employees and Manage Assignments is the value stream's confirmed specialist-role gap: HR Business Partner Manager's ownership here is explicitly partial, since International HR Management is a recognized specialist discipline the current role catalog does not name.
