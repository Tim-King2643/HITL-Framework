Description

This value stream comprises one APQC PCF process group: 9.6 Process Accounts Payable and Expense Reimbursements — spanning 3 Level 3 processes and 22 Level 4 activities.

At the activity level, this value stream's work breaks down as 16 Transaction activities — the highest concentration of any PCF 9.0 value stream — 3 Decision activities, 2 Document activities, and 1 Exception activity.

At M3 (HITL Design Intent), the value stream's aggregate human-involvement ratio is 44%, down from a 67% undesigned baseline — a 23-point AI convergence opportunity.

Design Narrative

Procure-to-Pay follows the same 2-of-3 pattern as Time-to-Pay: Director of Accounting holds Accountable for accounts payable (9.6.1) and expense reimbursements (9.6.2), while Treasury Manager owns the stream's third L3, corporate credit card management (9.6.3) — a natural Treasury carve-out since card programs are banking-relationship products even when the resulting spend flows through Accounting's payable process. This value stream carries the domain's highest concentration of Transaction-pattern activity (16 of 22 activities), reflecting how much of accounts-payable work is rules-based processing once a vendor invoice or expense claim is submitted, and it also carries one of PCF 9.0's four Exception-pattern activities (9.6.1.7, Research/Resolve payable exceptions). Its steepest AI-absorption opportunity sits in 9.6.2.1 (Establish and communicate expense reimbursement policies and approval limits, Document-pattern, -32 points).

Handoff Points

Procure-to-Pay opens with Director of Accounting-accountable accounts payable (9.6.1) — matching, auditing, and approving vendor invoices for payment.

Expense reimbursements (9.6.2) run as a parallel Director of Accounting-owned lane for employee-submitted claims rather than vendor invoices, closing with the same approve-and-pay pattern.

Corporate credit card management (9.6.3) hands to Treasury Manager, who owns card issuance and limit management even though resulting transactions still settle through Director of Accounting's payable process.
