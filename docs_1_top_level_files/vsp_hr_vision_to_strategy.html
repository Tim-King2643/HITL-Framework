Description

This value stream comprises one APQC PCF process group: 7.1 Develop and Manage HR Planning, Policies, and Strategies — spanning 4 Level 3 processes and 31 Level 4 activities.

At the activity level, this value stream's work breaks down as 23 Knowledge activities (judgment, synthesis, and interpretation-driven work), 4 Document activities (content generation), and 4 Transaction activities (rules-based processing suited to automation). No Decision- or Exception-pattern activity exists within this value stream.

At M3 (HITL Design Intent), the value stream's aggregate human-involvement ratio is 58%, down from an 80% undesigned baseline — a 22-point AI convergence opportunity.

Design Narrative

HR Vision-to-Strategy is the framework's only Executive-tier value stream in PCF 7.0: 7.1.1 (Develop human resources strategy) is the domain's single Executive-Accountable L3, with CHRO holding formal Accountability while HRBP Manager and Director of HR Ops carry the Responsible drafting work. Director of HR Ops then holds Accountable for translating that strategy into workforce planning and keeping it current (7.1.2, 7.1.3), while Director of L&D owns competency model design (7.1.4) as an adjacent step. Every other PCF 7.0 value stream operates downstream of this one — its outputs (approved strategy, workforce plans, policies, competency models) are the direct inputs the operational and enablement streams execute against. Its steepest AI-absorption opportunities cluster in Document-pattern content — 7.1.1.7 (communicating HR strategy) and 7.1.2.16 (maintaining job descriptions) both sit at -32 points.

Handoff Points

HR Vision-to-Strategy opens with CHRO-accountable strategy formation (7.1.1), then hands to Director of HR Ops for workforce planning, policy administration, and ongoing strategy monitoring (7.1.2, 7.1.3) — the value stream's design-and-governance layer. Director of L&D holds competency management models (7.1.4) as a single adjacent step within this same design-tier group, closing the value stream before its outputs hand off downstream to every operational and enablement stream in the domain.
