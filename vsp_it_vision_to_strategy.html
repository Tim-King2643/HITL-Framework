Description

This value stream comprises one APQC PCF process group: 8.2 Develop and Manage IT Business Strategy — spanning 7 Level 3 processes and 43 Level 4 activities.

At the activity level, this value stream's work breaks down as 30 Knowledge activities (judgment, synthesis, and interpretation-driven work), 7 Transaction activities (rules-based processing suited to automation), 4 Document activities (content generation), and 2 Decision activities (judgment-and-authority gates that stay human by design). No Exception-pattern activity exists within this value stream.

At M3 (HITL Design Intent), the value stream's aggregate human-involvement ratio is 59%, down from an 81% undesigned baseline — a 22-point AI convergence opportunity.

Design Narrative

IT Vision-to-Strategy is one of three Executive-Accountable apex streams in PCF 8.0 rather than the domain's only one — 8.2.1 (Define business technology and governance strategy) is CIO's own strategy-formation L3, distinct from CISO's security-strategy apex (Risk-to-Resilience, 8.3.1) and CDO's data-strategy apex (Data-to-Insight, 8.4.1), since IT's taxonomy gives each Executive role its own explicit governance mandate rather than concentrating all domain strategy under one CHRO-equivalent as HR does. IT Director then holds Accountable for the stream's cross-cutting management activities — portfolio strategy (8.2.2), IT management-system control (8.2.5), and value-portfolio management (8.2.6) — while Enterprise Architect owns the two most technically specialized L3s, enterprise architecture itself (8.2.3) and technology innovation (8.2.7). IT Service Manager's single L3 (8.2.4, ITSM strategy) is the stream's one specialist-owned exception to the IT Director/Enterprise Architect split. Its steepest AI-absorption opportunity sits in 8.2.1.5 (Articulate IT alignment principles, Document-pattern, -32 points).

Handoff Points

IT Vision-to-Strategy opens with CIO-accountable strategy formation (8.2.1), then hands to IT Director for portfolio strategy, management-system control, and value-portfolio management (8.2.2, 8.2.5, 8.2.6) — the stream's cross-cutting governance layer.

Enterprise Architect picks up the stream's most technically specialized work: enterprise architecture definition (8.2.3) and technology innovation (8.2.7), both requiring deep technical judgment distinct from IT Director's portfolio-management lane.

IT Service Manager holds a single adjacent step — ITSM strategy definition (8.2.4) — closing the stream before its outputs (approved technology strategy, portfolio priorities, architecture standards) hand off to every operational stream in the domain.
