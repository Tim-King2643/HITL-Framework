Description

This value stream comprises one APQC PCF process group: 8.5 Develop and Manage Services/Solutions — spanning 5 Level 3 processes and 22 Level 4 activities.

At the activity level, this value stream's work breaks down as 13 Knowledge activities (judgment, synthesis, and interpretation-driven work), 5 Transaction activities (rules-based processing suited to automation), 2 Document activities (content generation), 1 Decision activity, and 1 Exception activity.

At M3 (HITL Design Intent), the value stream's aggregate human-involvement ratio is 50%, down from a 73% undesigned baseline — a 23-point AI convergence opportunity.

Design Narrative

Solution-to-Build splits ownership along a design/execution line: Enterprise Architect holds Accountable for the stream's three design-facing L3s — development strategy (8.5.1), lifecycle planning (8.5.2), and solution architecture itself (8.5.3) — while DevOps Engineer and Systems Administrator hold Accountable directly for the two execution-facing L3s, service/solution creation and testing (8.5.4) and maintenance and testing (8.5.5), with no Managerial-tier owner in between. This is the same Operational-tier-Accountable pattern seen in PCF 7.0's Recruit-to-Hire: these are genuinely hands-on build/test/maintain activities where Enterprise Architect owns the blueprint but not the build. Its steepest AI-absorption opportunity sits in 8.5.1.2 (Define IT service/solution development processes/standards, Document-pattern, -32 points).

Handoff Points

The stream opens with Enterprise Architect owning the design-facing work: development strategy (8.5.1), lifecycle planning (8.5.2), and solution architecture itself (8.5.3).

It then hands to execution: DevOps Engineer picks up service/solution creation and testing (8.5.4) with Enterprise Architect retained as Consulted, and Systems Administrator closes the stream with maintenance and testing (8.5.5) — the design-to-build handoff this value stream is organized around.
