Data-to-Insight

PCF 8.4 · Information & analytics strategy, architecture & lifecycle

4 PCF processes · 14 L4 activities · M1 73% → M3 50% (Δ -23%)

⇓ Download as PDF

Description

This value stream comprises one APQC PCF process group: 8.4 Manage Information — spanning 4 Level 3 processes and 14 Level 4 activities.

At the activity level, this value stream's work breaks down as 6 Transaction activities (rules-based processing suited to automation), 5 Knowledge activities (judgment, synthesis, and interpretation-driven work), 2 Decision activities (judgment-and-authority gates that stay human by design), and 1 Document activity (content generation). No Exception-pattern activity exists within this value stream.

At M3 (HITL Design Intent), the value stream's aggregate human-involvement ratio is 50%, down from a 73% undesigned baseline — a 23-point AI convergence opportunity.

Design Narrative

Data-to-Insight follows the same apex pattern as IT Vision-to-Strategy and Risk-to-Resilience: 8.4.1 (Define business information and analytics strategy) is Chief Data Officer's own strategy-formation L3, while Director of Data & Analytics holds Accountable for the remaining 3 L3s — information architecture (8.4.2), lifecycle planning and control (8.4.3), and ongoing information management (8.4.4). Data Engineer carries Responsible across all 4 L3s without ever holding Accountable, the cleanest single-role execution lane of any PCF 8.0 value stream. This is also the only value stream where Enterprise Architect appears solely as Consulted rather than Accountable anywhere — information architecture here is Director of Data & Analytics' domain, with Enterprise Architect's involvement scoped to alignment with the broader enterprise architecture rather than ownership. Its steepest AI-absorption opportunity sits in 8.4.3.1 (Define and maintain enterprise information policies, standards, and procedures, Document-pattern, -32 points).

Handoff Points

The stream opens with Chief Data Officer-accountable strategy formation (8.4.1), then hands to Director of Data & Analytics for the remaining three L3s: information architecture (8.4.2), lifecycle planning and control (8.4.3), and ongoing information management (8.4.4).

Data Engineer carries Responsible across all four L3s, the cleanest single-execution-role lane in PCF 8.0 — the handoff from Director of Data & Analytics to Data Engineer is consistent throughout rather than shifting by L3.
