Description

This value stream comprises one APQC PCF process group: 9.7 Manage Treasury Operations — spanning 6 Level 3 processes and 38 Level 4 activities, PCF 9.0's second-largest value stream by both measures, behind Record-to-Report.

At the activity level, this value stream's work breaks down as 20 Transaction activities, 14 Knowledge activities — the highest concentration of any PCF 9.0 value stream — 2 Document activities, 1 Decision activity, and 1 Exception activity.

At M3 (HITL Design Intent), the value stream's aggregate human-involvement ratio is 54%, down from a 75% undesigned baseline — a 21-point AI convergence opportunity, tied with Acquire-to-Retire as the shallowest in PCF 9.0.

Design Narrative

Treasury & Risk is VP Treasury's stream in the same way Order-to-Cash is Director of Accounting's: Accountable for 5 of the stream's 6 L3s — treasury policy (9.7.1), cash management (9.7.2), in-house banking (9.7.3), debt and investment (9.7.4), and risk/hedging (9.7.5) — with only financial fraud/dispute case management (9.7.6) carved out to Internal Audit Manager as a compliance-investigation specialty. Despite VP Treasury holding Executive tier and Accountable ownership of the stream's own policy-setting L3 (9.7.1), this value stream classifies Operational rather than Strategy: the work itself — cash positioning, in-house banking, debt/investment execution, and hedge transactions — is treasury operations, not strategy formation, the same reasoning that keeps PCF 8.0's CISO-owned Risk-to-Resilience in Enablement rather than Strategy. This is PCF 9.0's second-largest value stream by L3 count (6, behind Record-to-Report's 7) and by activity count (38, behind Record-to-Report's 42), with the domain's highest concentration of Knowledge-pattern work (14 of 38 activities) — cash-flow forecasting, banking-relationship management, and hedging strategy all demand financial judgment that resists pure automation. Its steepest AI-absorption opportunity sits in 9.7.1.2 (Establish and publish treasury policies, Document-pattern, -32 points).

Handoff Points

Treasury & Risk opens with VP Treasury-accountable policy setting and governance scope (9.7.1), then runs cash management (9.7.2) and in-house banking (9.7.3) as the stream's core daily-operations lane.

Debt and investment management (9.7.4) and risk/hedging (9.7.5) run as VP Treasury's higher-judgment lane — managing liquidity, issuer exposure, and hedge strategy — consuming market data and cash-position outputs from the operations lane.

Financial fraud and dispute case management (9.7.6) is the stream's one carve-out to Internal Audit Manager, engaged whenever a cash, banking, or payment-fraud exception surfaces from any of VP Treasury's other four lanes.
