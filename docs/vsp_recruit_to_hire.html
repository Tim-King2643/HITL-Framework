Description

This value stream comprises one APQC PCF process group: 7.2 Recruit, Source, and Select Employees — spanning 5 Level 3 processes and 24 Level 4 activities.

At the activity level, this value stream's work breaks down as 15 Transaction activities (rules-based processing suited to automation), 5 Knowledge activities (judgment, synthesis, and interpretation-driven work), 3 Decision activities (judgment-and-authority gates that stay human by design), and 1 Document activity (content generation). No Exception-pattern activity exists within this value stream.

Under the Working-Relationship model, this value stream’s aggregate Current State sits at H 38%; its designed Future State brings that to H 31% (Δ -7 points) — 8 of 24 activities have not yet reached their Future State target, and 15 have already reached their Progression Ceiling with no further AI-absorption headroom remaining.

Design Narrative

Recruit-to-Hire, now scoped to candidate acquisition alone, keeps human judgment concentrated at two points: candidate selection itself (7.2.3, the interview and select/reject decision) and final hire commitment (7.2.4, offer negotiation) — both Accountable to Recruiter. Everything else in the value stream — requisition administration (7.2.1), sourcing-channel execution (7.2.2), applicant data management (7.2.5) — has been reassessed under the Working-Relationship model. Its steepest gaps are actually two Oversight-classified gates — 7.2.1.3 (Approve job requisition) and 7.2.4.3 (Hire candidate) — each a 34-point gap to an Agent-delegation ceiling, once a standing accountability duty (budget authority, compensation commitment) stops holding them at a higher tier but before AI can execute the step outright. 7.2.4.1 (Draw up and make offer), previously flagged as the steepest gap under the old model, carries a real but shallower 18-point gap. 7.2.5, by contrast, is already fully realized: all four of its activities sit at their Automation ceiling today, with no further AI-absorption headroom remaining there.

Handoff Points

Recruit-to-Hire opens with HRBP Manager on requisition management (7.2.1), then hands to Recruiter for the full candidate lifecycle: sourcing (7.2.2), screening and selection (7.2.3), and new hire/offer management (7.2.4) — the value stream's largest single lane.

The funnel closes with HRIS Analyst, Accountable for applicant information management (7.2.5) — background checks, records, tracking, archiving. All four activities here are already at their Automation ceiling — the cleanest fully-realized lane in the value stream, not an outstanding AI-absorption opportunity.
