Description

This value stream comprises one APQC PCF process group: 7.2 Recruit, Source, and Select Employees — spanning 5 Level 3 processes and 24 Level 4 activities.

At the activity level, this value stream's work breaks down as 15 Transaction activities (rules-based processing suited to automation), 5 Knowledge activities (judgment, synthesis, and interpretation-driven work), 3 Decision activities (judgment-and-authority gates that stay human by design), and 1 Document activity (content generation). No Exception-pattern activity exists within this value stream.

At M3 (HITL Design Intent), the value stream's aggregate human-involvement ratio is 47%, down from a 70% undesigned baseline — a 23-point AI convergence opportunity.

Design Narrative

Recruit-to-Hire, now scoped to candidate acquisition alone, keeps human judgment concentrated at two points: candidate selection itself (7.2.3, the interview and select/reject decision) and final hire commitment (7.2.4, offer negotiation) — both Accountable to Recruiter. Everything else in the value stream — requisition administration (7.2.1), sourcing-channel execution (7.2.2), applicant data management (7.2.5) — carries real AI-absorption opportunity at M3, reflected in the value stream's steepest single gap sitting in 7.2.4.1 (Draw up and make offer, Document-pattern, -32 points), with 7.2.5's entirely Transaction-pattern applicant-information work close behind at -25.

Handoff Points

Recruit-to-Hire opens with HRBP Manager on requisition management (7.2.1), then hands to Recruiter for the full candidate lifecycle: sourcing (7.2.2), screening and selection (7.2.3), and new hire/offer management (7.2.4) — the value stream's largest single lane.

The funnel closes with HRIS Analyst, Accountable for applicant information management (7.2.5) — background checks, records, tracking, archiving — entirely Transaction-pattern, one of the value stream's largest AI-absorption opportunities (Δ -25 points).
