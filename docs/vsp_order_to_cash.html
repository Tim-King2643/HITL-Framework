Description

This value stream comprises one APQC PCF process group: 9.2 Perform Revenue Accounting — spanning 5 Level 3 processes and 30 Level 4 activities.

At the activity level, this value stream's work breaks down as 13 Transaction activities, 6 Knowledge activities, 6 Document activities, 4 Decision activities (judgment-and-authority gates that stay human by design), and 1 Exception activity — a non-standard case requiring human resolution.

At M3 (HITL Design Intent), the value stream's aggregate human-involvement ratio is 48%, down from a 72% undesigned baseline — a 24-point AI convergence opportunity.

Design Narrative

Order-to-Cash is the cleanest single-owner value stream in PCF 9.0: Director of Accounting holds Accountable for all 5 of the stream's L3s — customer credit (9.2.1), invoicing (9.2.2), accounts receivable (9.2.3), collections (9.2.4), and adjustments/deductions (9.2.5). AP/AR Specialist carries Responsible across nearly all of that work, a consistent Managerial-owns/Operational-executes pattern throughout. This value stream also carries one of PCF 9.0's four Exception-pattern activities — 9.2.4.7 (Manage default accounts) — reflecting how collections work inevitably produces cases that fall outside standard policy. Its steepest AI-absorption opportunity sits in 9.2.1.1 (Establish credit policies, Document-pattern, -32 points).

Handoff Points

Order-to-Cash opens with credit-policy setting and account approval (9.2.1), then hands to invoicing (9.2.2) once a customer account is cleared to transact.

Invoicing output feeds accounts receivable processing (9.2.3) — payment application and AR reporting — which in turn feeds collections (9.2.4) for any balance that ages past terms.

Adjustments/deductions (9.2.5) runs alongside collections as its own lane, closing out chargebacks and disputed amounts before the stream's outputs (aging reports, AR ledger) hand off to Record-to-Report's general-ledger posting.
