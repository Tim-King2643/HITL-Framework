A Named Case Study, Not a Hypothetical

This is the smallest role profiled in the framework, but it's also the only one whose exact function Josh Bersin names as a live, real-world deployment rather than a projected trend. In the same January 2026 piece already sourced for Compensation Analyst, Bersin describes his firm's own client work using the autonomous-car analogy for what he calls Superagents — systems that manage an entire process outcome, not just assist an individual worker — and states plainly: “We are already working with a large insurance company, an airline, and a pharma company on Superagents for global onboarding, targeted talent acquisition, and sales training.”

Global onboarding is one of only three named, currently-deployed Superagent case studies in that entire piece. For this role, that changes the nature of the transition question: it isn't whether onboarding gets a Superagent, but what this role's job becomes once the checklist-execution layer (this role's own Transaction-pattern activity) is the part actually running inside one.

Skills & Competencies

Bersin's autonomous-car analogy is worth taking literally here: the purpose of the car is to optimize the passenger's journey, not to make the driver's job easier — and “suddenly the driver 'goes away,' similar to replacing a routine job.” The skills below follow from what's left once the checklist-execution driver seat is automated.

  • Program ownership at the outcome level — this role already holds sole Accountability for onboarding; a Superagent world makes that ownership about outcomes (retention, time-to-productivity) rather than task completion
  • Superagent oversight — Bersin's own case studies involve his firm working directly alongside the companies deploying these systems, not a hands-off rollout; this role is the natural human owner of that relationship
  • New-hire experience judgment — the Knowledge-pattern majority of this role's real scope (developing and monitoring the program) is exactly what determines whether an automated onboarding experience actually lands well
  • Business-metric fluency — once the process runs autonomously, success has to be measured in ramp-time and retention, not checklist completion
  • Comfort with a genuinely small, deep scope — this role's entire footprint is one process; its transition path is depth in that one process, not breadth across many

Framework connection: The Role Impact Snapshot's Knowledge-majority (67%) finding matches what stays once a Superagent absorbs this role's one Transaction-pattern activity — the program design and monitoring work is exactly the ownership layer Bersin's own client deployments still need a human for.
