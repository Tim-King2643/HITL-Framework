Description

This value stream comprises 4 Level 3 processes drawn from APQC PCF process group 8.7 Create and Manage Support Services/Solutions — the execution-facing half of that group — spanning 21 Level 4 activities.

At the activity level, this value stream's work breaks down as 15 Transaction activities (rules-based processing suited to automation), 5 Exception activities (non-standard resolution, the highest concentration of any PCF 8.0 value stream), and 1 Knowledge activity. No Decision- or Document-pattern activity exists within this value stream.

At M3 (HITL Design Intent), the value stream's aggregate human-involvement ratio is 51%, down from a 73% undesigned baseline — a 22-point AI convergence opportunity.

Design Narrative

Support-to-Resolve is the execution-facing half of the former Request-to-Fulfill stream: Systems Administrator holds Accountable for 3 of 4 L3s — delivery control (8.7.3), delivery operations (8.7.6), and infrastructure resource administration (8.7.7) — direct hands-on infrastructure work, with Network Engineer carrying Responsible on two of those three. IT Support Specialist closes the stream with end-user support (8.7.8), the domain's Tier 1/2 support lane. This value stream carries PCF 8.0's highest concentration of Exception-pattern work (5 of 21 activities, all clustered in unplanned-issue response and IT support triage/escalation/resolution) and zero Knowledge- or Document-pattern content beyond a single activity — the most operationally procedural value stream in the domain. Its steepest AI-absorption opportunity sits in 8.7.6.1 (Operate and monitor online systems, Transaction-pattern, -25 points).

Handoff Points

The stream opens with Systems Administrator on delivery control (8.7.3), then continues through delivery operations (8.7.6) and infrastructure resource administration (8.7.7) — Network Engineer carries Responsible on the latter two, a consistent Managerial-tier-Accountable/Operational-tier-execution pattern.

It closes with IT Support Specialist on end-user support (8.7.8) — triage, escalation, and resolution of IT issues and requests, the domain's Tier 1/2 support lane and the stream's highest concentration of Exception-pattern work.
