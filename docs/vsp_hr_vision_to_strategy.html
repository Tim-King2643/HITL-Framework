Description

This value stream comprises one APQC PCF process group: 7.1 Develop and Manage HR Planning, Policies, and Strategies — spanning 4 Level 3 processes and 31 Level 4 activities.

At the activity level, this value stream's work breaks down as 23 Knowledge activities (judgment, synthesis, and interpretation-driven work), 4 Document activities (content generation), and 4 Transaction activities (rules-based processing suited to automation). No Decision- or Exception-pattern activity exists within this value stream.

Under the Working-Relationship model, this value stream’s aggregate Current State sits at H 58%; its designed Future State brings that to H 50% (Δ -8 points) — 12 of 31 activities have not yet reached their Future State target, and 19 have already reached their Progression Ceiling with no further AI-absorption headroom remaining.

Design Narrative

HR Vision-to-Strategy is the framework's only Executive-tier value stream in PCF 7.0: 7.1.1 (Develop human resources strategy) is the domain's single Executive-Accountable L3, with CHRO holding formal Accountability while HRBP Manager and Director of HR Ops carry the Responsible drafting work. Director of HR Ops then holds Accountable for translating that strategy into workforce planning and keeping it current (7.1.2, 7.1.3), while Director of L&D owns competency model design (7.1.4) as an adjacent step. Every other PCF 7.0 value stream operates downstream of this one — its outputs (approved strategy, workforce plans, policies, competency models) are the direct inputs the operational and enablement streams execute against. Under real per-activity assessment, its steepest gap is 7.1.1.5 (Determine HR costs), currently classified Oversight against an Agent-delegation ceiling — a 34-point H% gap, the widest in this value stream. 7.1.1.7 (communicating HR strategy) carries a real but shallower 20-point gap to the same ceiling; 7.1.2.16 (maintaining job descriptions), previously flagged under the old model, is already at its Augmentation ceiling with no further AI-absorption headroom remaining.

Handoff Points

HR Vision-to-Strategy opens with CHRO-accountable strategy formation (7.1.1), then hands to Director of HR Ops for workforce planning, policy administration, and ongoing strategy monitoring (7.1.2, 7.1.3) — the value stream's design-and-governance layer. Director of L&D holds competency management models (7.1.4) as a single adjacent step within this same design-tier group, closing the value stream before its outputs hand off downstream to every operational and enablement stream in the domain.
