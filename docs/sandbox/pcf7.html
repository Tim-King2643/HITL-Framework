Readiness Gate — PCF 7.0

Per Working_Relationship_Progression_Methodology.docx §5 — an activity advances toward its Future State only once all three independent axes clear, gated at L3 via its RACI Accountable role
None of the three axes has a real instrument yet. Technical/Process and Organizational-level have no instrument anywhere in the framework (per §5/§9). Role-level is meant to reuse the AI Transition Pathways self-assessment / TMI tier output, joined by this L3’s Accountable role — but those per-role results haven’t been ported into this sandbox yet. Every cell below is an honest blank, not a zero — this view exists to hold the shape of the gate, not to imply readiness has been measured.
