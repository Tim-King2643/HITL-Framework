Sandbox PCF 7.0 — Develop and Manage Human Resources. This is a live working copy of the full PCF 7.0 content in four views (GR-059): Work, People, Data and Findings. It is the framework’s Reference Model: for each of the 118 activities, Work shows the Human-only baseline and the Progression Ceiling, which is how far AI may take the activity, on the full six-step ramp, with what changes at each step, the activity’s decision point and its Consequence of Error. Choose an organization at the top to see its assessed Current State instead, and open any activity to assess it. The process structure, RACI, and CRUD data are the framework’s real PCF 7.0 data. Ceilings, Consequence-of-Error ratings, and every % H figure are modeled — reasoned judgments for Tim and GiGi to pressure-test, not measured observations. Leave a reaction or a note on any row using the Notes button; GiGi and Tim can both see and add to them here.
