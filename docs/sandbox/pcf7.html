Sandbox PCF 7.0 — Develop and Manage Human Resources. This is a live working copy of the full PCF 7.0 content — Working-Relationship Table, RACI Table, Data View (CRUD Matrix), and People View. The Working-Relationship Table shows each activity's Current State → Progression Ceiling plotted on the full six-step ramp, with the Per-Transition Evidence Ladder as clickable gates in between, plus Consequence of Error, for the 118 activities that have been hand-classified by working relationship so far. The process structure, RACI, and CRUD data are the framework’s real PCF 7.0 data. Working-relationship classifications, Consequence-of-Error ratings, and every % H figure are modeled — reasoned judgments for Tim and GiGi to pressure-test, not measured observations. Leave a reaction or a note on any row using the Notes button; GiGi and Tim can both see and add to them here.

Readiness Gate — PCF 7.0

Per Working_Relationship_Progression_Methodology.docx §5 — an activity advances toward its Progression Ceiling only once all three independent axes clear, gated at L3 via its RACI Accountable role
None of the three axes has a real instrument yet. Technical/Process and Organizational-level have no instrument anywhere in the framework (per §5/§9). Role-level is meant to reuse the AI Transition Pathways self-assessment / TMI tier output, joined by this L3’s Accountable role — but those per-role results haven’t been ported into this sandbox yet.
