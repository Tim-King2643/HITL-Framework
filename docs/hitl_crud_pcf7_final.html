7.0 — Develop and Manage Human Resources · Domain-wide, all 8 process groups

This page maps every data entity each process Creates, Reads, Updates, or Deletes, and the system of record it lives in, for all of 7.0 (Develop and Manage Human Resources) — 8 process groups, 37 Level 3 processes, and 118 Level 4 activities in total. It's the Data View's answer to a different question than Process View (how much stays human) or People View (who's accountable): what actually gets touched, and where does it live.

CRUD assignment in this framework is confirmed at L3 process granularity and inherited by L4 children, the same convention already used for RACI — never assigned independently per L4. Coverage is real, not exhaustive: only L3s with a confirmed CRUD assignment show entries; the rest show “·”, an honest gap rather than an inferred one. 37 of 37 L3 processes have confirmed CRUD data so far.
