Organizational Role Taxonomy

The People-side counterpart to PCF — a normalized role catalog for PCF 7.0 (Develop and Manage Human Resources)

The People Side of this framework mirrors the Business Side's PCF structure with a role-based lens: instead of asking how AI reshapes a value stream, it asks how AI reshapes the specific job of the person doing the work. The taxonomy below is the anchor for that lens — a normalized, three-tier role catalog aligned to APQC PCF v8.0, scoped here to Domain 7.0 (Develop and Manage Human Resources), the same domain covered by every Value Stream Profile on the Business Side.

Reference Baseline, Not a Binding Structure

This taxonomy is a normalized cross-industry baseline. Role titles, reporting lines, and tier assignments should be adapted to reflect each organization's grading structure and nomenclature. Tiers reflect accountability levels, not compensation grades.

Three Tiers

Executive (E)

C-suite executives, SVPs, and VPs holding strategic accountability for domain outcomes, enterprise resource allocation, and board-level reporting.

Managerial (M)

Directors, Senior Managers, and Managers with operational ownership of domain-specific functions, team leadership, and cross-functional coordination.

Operational (O)

Specialists, Analysts, Coordinators, and Associates responsible for day-to-day execution, delivery, and process-level activity.

On the Reporting Structure

The reportsTo edges above are a reasoned first-pass structure — inferred from typical HR org design and this framework's own RACI signals, not sourced from a named external authority (no public body publishes org-chart-level detail at this granularity). A self-consistency check against real RACI Consulted/Informed data found strong support for 5 of 8 Managerial-tier edges; 2 edges (Director of L&D and HRBP Manager's claimed reporting lines) showed no support in the RACI data, with a specific alternative candidate surfaced instead. This structure is provisional and awaiting confirmation from a People-side subject matter expert.

Role Transition Profiles

11 of the 14 roles have a completed Role Transition Profile — a role-specific companion to the Business Side's Value Stream Profiles, showing what the framework's HITL maturity data means for that specific job. The remaining 3 (SVP Talent Management, VP People & Culture, Director of Talent Acquisition) currently compute to zero Responsible/Accountable touches under the framework's strict RACI rule; their transition stories are shaped by span of control rather than process pattern-mix, which this doc type's current methodology can't yet produce. They stay held until that reporting-line data is confirmed.
