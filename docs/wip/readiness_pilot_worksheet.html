Readiness pilot worksheet

GiGi’s v0.2 instrument, unchanged, set up to run one pilot by hand. Each dimension card gives her readiness question and the evidence to look for, the work-side facts the sandbox already holds, and her standard record to fill in.

Work-side facts are Modeled, from the Reference Model. They are context for the assessor, never a finding: every finding starts at Insufficient Evidence and moves only on organization-specific evidence (v0.2 section 5).
