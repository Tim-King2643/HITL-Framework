ABC Test Org: a complete data set for 7.2

One worked example of every input the sandbox takes, across the 24 activities of 7.2 Recruit, Source and Select, so each part of the Work, People, Data and Findings views has something real to show. Each activity carries one scenario, chosen so every outcome appears at least once.

Loading saves the set through the sandbox’s own endpoints, under your sign-in, exactly as if each record were entered by hand. It replaces ABC Test Org’s current 7.2 data; every earlier entry stays in its record’s history. Nothing outside 7.2 is touched.

The 24 scenarios

What each activity shows once loaded. The outcome is the Readiness Gate for its next step, as Findings’ Readiness dashboard reads it.

Where each input shows

  • Stage assessment and element grid: Work, the Current State step; open Reassess on any assessed activity. Evidence types and confidence vary across the set.
  • Unassessed (7.2.1.6, 7.2.5.3): dashed squares in Work and Findings.
  • Element past its own ceiling (7.2.2.4): red dot in Work; Findings map and Exceptions report.
  • Past the ceiling, no override (7.2.2.2) and ceiling override with a review (7.2.2.1): Work and the Findings summary.
  • Readiness Gate: Work, the arrow before the next step. Role readiness by weakest link on High and Critical work (7.2.1.3, 7.2.3.4), by role below High (7.2.2.4), governance oversight (7.2.1.3, 7.2.3.2, 7.2.3.3, 7.2.3.4, 7.2.5.1), required actions recorded or missing, Human Agency narratives per role.
  • Step evidence: Met, Not met by a rate (7.2.1.2, 7.2.5.1), Not met by an open Serious entry (7.2.2.6), Insufficient on volume (7.2.1.5), sign-off on High work and Automation, discrepancy logs with resolved and open entries.
  • Findings: the Readiness dashboard shows every gate outcome; “What’s holding the work” ranks what holds them.
