As AI takes on more of the work, the work a person still does changes shape: from doing every instance, to approving every instance, to handling only the ones AI flags. Accountability doesn’t move with the work. This sketch moves every activity up the ramp and shows, role by role, what is left.

How it is counted

The role doing the work is the Responsible role, or the Accountable role where no human role is Responsible (35 activities). At Human-only, Judgment and Augmentation that role is in every instance; at Oversight the approver is; at Agent-delegation the role doing the work handles the flagged instances, and High and Critical ones go where the setting says; at Automation no one is in the instance. The Accountable role stays answerable at every stage.

These are counts of activities, not hours or volumes, so they show where the work lands, not how much of it there is.
