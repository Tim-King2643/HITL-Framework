Readiness in the Readiness Gate

In the Work view, clicking a step on an activity’s ramp shows the Readiness Gate’s three checks. Two of them read “Not yet measured (GR-012)”. This sketch fills them from GiGi’s v0.2 instrument and brings the pilot worksheet into the sandbox.

The rules, recommended
  1. Role readiness reads her six role dimensions; Organizational readiness reads her three organizational dimensions. Trust / Evidence Quality, her shared layer, shows under both.
  2. On High or Critical work each dimension rolls up the required roles by weakest link (BR-16); a single required role is read directly. The oversight above is assessed separately and shown beside, never rolled in.
  3. Each row names its weakest dimension and the role holding it back, with the count by finding. No score (v0.2).
  4. The step holds while any dimension is Not Ready or Insufficient Evidence (gray is never converted). It clears with conditions while any is Conditional, listing them; it clears when all are Ready. Candidate hard gates are tagged; they stay provisional.
  5. Readiness is assessed per organization and saved with its assessments. The Reference Model shows the gate as assessed per organization: readiness cannot be modeled.
  6. Sign-off follows the finding-governance rule: the Accountable owner plus the assessor on Low or Moderate work; a cross-functional panel on High or Critical.
