Process RACI from the activities

Each process’s RACI is worked out from the RACI of its activities (ACTIVITY_RACI, confirmed Oct 15), so it can no longer drift from them. Each card compares the result with the process RACI stored today.

The rule
  1. Owner (Accountable). The role Accountable for most of the process’s activities, as approved in the draft. On a tie, the role Accountable for the activity with the highest Consequence of Error; if still tied, the more senior role.
  2. Also Accountable. Any other role Accountable for some of its activities, with the count.
  3. Responsible, Consulted, Informed. Every other role, under the strongest letter it holds on any activity (Responsible over Consulted over Informed), with the count.
  4. Outside participants (Legal*, Finance*, Hiring Managers*) are listed as they appear on the activities.

Applying it

The 37 stored process RACIs are removed from pcf7.html and the process RACI is computed wherever it is shown, as process CRUD already is. ACTIVITY_RACI is added to the change-log audit, so an activity RACI edit cannot go in without a change-log entry. No activity’s RACI changes.
