Who needs a judgment-building path

Agreed Nov 5 (GR-019): when AI takes the work people learned on, the learning has to be replaced on purpose. A role needs a path when both are true:

  1. It is Responsible for activities whose Progression Ceiling is Agent-delegation or Automation: work moving to AI.
  2. It keeps a High or Critical decision: it is Accountable or Responsible on a High or Critical activity whose control element is Decide and is still held by a person at the ceiling.

The certificate

The path is individual development, envisioned as a Judgment Certificate. Moving into Decide independently follows the finding-governance rule: the Accountable role on the decision signs it. Where the role is itself Accountable, the role it reports to signs.

Not a gate: a missing path is evidence for a Conditional finding on Capability/Skill (GR-012).

Applying it

Recommended: the path is required for Operational roles, where people enter the profession and learn on the work that is moving. Managerial and Executive roles are listed for review: their own judgment was built before, but they sign the certificates and their pipeline depends on it.
