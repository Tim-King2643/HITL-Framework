Page 1 — Human Readiness Gate

GiGi's eight readiness dimensions, transcribed as a plain reusable checklist. Cited by name in Page 3's worked examples.

For a given activity, ask whether the person actually named Accountable or Responsible has each of these — not whether they hold the title. Rate each weak / adequate / strong with a short note on why. This is a diagnostic, not a score.

The pressure test keeps coming back to the same gap: a person can be named Accountable and still not be able to exercise that accountability. I want to make that visible.— GiGi Garcia, HITL Working Thoughts & Next Steps
DimensionQuestion
1. AuthorityCan they accept, reject, pause, or override the AI?
2. InformationDo they have enough context to make an independent judgment?
3. CapabilityDo they have the knowledge and experience for the remaining work?
4. CapacityDo they realistically have the time and bandwidth to exercise judgment?
5. Role clarityIs it obvious what the human owns and what the AI owns?
6. ConfidenceDo they understand the technology well enough to challenge it?
7. Psychological safetyCan they question or override the AI without organizational penalty?
8. Escalation / overrideIs there a clear, named path when the human can't resolve it?
WeakCannot meaningfully exercise this today — the gap is real and load-bearing.
AdequatePresent but bounded, partial, or untested.
StrongConfirmed and load-bearing — not where a transition would fail.

This doesn't replace the working-relationship classification or the consequence-of-error tag — see Page 2 for criticality, and Page 3 for the full chain applied to real activities.
