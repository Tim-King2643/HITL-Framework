Work Product in the Role Container

What a role produces, who relies on it, and what it must verify as AI takes a larger part
Documentation Dashboard Work in Progress
What this is

A role is the container; what changes inside it is the work product. This design shows, for each role, the work products it produces, who relies on them, and what it must verify when AI has had a hand in producing them — the “verification tax” GiGi named.

Decided
  • Sept 25: a work product takes its production mode from the activity that produces it; with several producers, the one furthest along the ramp is shown and named. Derived each time it is shown, never stored.
  • Sept 26: create, read, update and delete are recorded per activity, reasoned from the activity itself, not inherited from the process; a process’s CRUD is then derived from its activities.
  • Sept 26: the work-product catalog is master data (39 entries), and the HRBP Manager’s 23 activities have their own CRUD (CL-007).

Designs GR-046. Related: GR-029 (verification load is its first measure), GR-050 (the work-product catalog).

1. The problem

The framework shows which activities move along the ramp, but not where their output goes or who has to check it. AI-produced work does not remove effort; it moves it to whoever must confirm the output before acting on it. The Performance Review shows it: produced in 7.3.2, it feeds promotion (7.6.1), pay (7.5.1), labor relations (7.4.1) and retention (7.5.3), and the HRBP Manager is Accountable at the source and again at two of those decisions — so pays the tax twice.

2. The design

TermMeaning
Work productA named output of an activity that other activities use: a record, review, plan or report. Held in the work-product catalog.
Production modeHow it is produced: the producing activity’s working relationship. Creates and updates produce; reads and deletes do not.
ConsumerAn activity that reads it, with its Accountable role, working relationship and Consequence of Error.
Verification pointWhere an AI-touched work product meets a human decision that relies on it.
Verification loadHow many AI-touched work products a role relies on at verification points. A count of exposure, not hours.
Pays twiceA role Accountable both where a work product is produced and where it is used.
Redefinition thresholdThe share of a role’s work products at Agent-delegation or Automation past which the role itself needs redefining.

Rules. Production mode is derived from the producing activity each time it is shown, so one classification change, logged once, updates every work product it produces. An unclassified producer or an undecided ceiling shows as a gap, never as human-only. A read is a verification point when the producer is at Augmentation or further, and the reader is at Judgment or Oversight or carries a High or Critical Consequence of Error (proposed).

Reference and organization. In the Reference Model every Current State is Human-only, so production mode and verification load are read at the Progression Ceiling: where checks would fall at full progression. With an organization selected they are read from its assessed Current State: where checks fall today (the rule in the reference model spec).

What a role shows, in an expandable panel under it in the People View: Produces, Relied on by, Must verify, and its verification load with the pays-twice marker. Every derived count carries the MODELED chip.

3. What is built

  • The work-product catalog: 39 entries, generic systems of record, master data in the sandbox.
  • Activity-level CRUD for the HRBP Manager’s 11 processes (23 activities), with those processes’ CRUD derived from them; the Data View shows each activity’s own CRUD (HRBP Manager CRUD).
  • Not yet: activity-level CRUD for the other roles; the role panel, work-product chips, trace view and verification-load indicator.

4. Open questions

Verification-point rule — Tim and GiGi

Adopt the proposed rule above, or revise it.

Consequence weighting — Tim and GiGi

Whether a review feeding a promotion counts more than one feeding a newsletter.

Redefinition threshold — Tim and GiGi

The rule is defined; the number is not.

Effort, not just exposure

Turning verification load into hours needs observed data the framework does not have.
