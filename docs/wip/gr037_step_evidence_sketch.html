Evidence to take a step

The Readiness Gate has three checks. Role and Organizational readiness are GiGi’s instrument. This is the third, Technical / process: what evidence must exist before an activity actually moves to the next working relationship (GR-037).

The evidence is gathered before moving: the current stage records its own audit trail, and the next stage is shadowed without taking effect.

The rules, recommended
  1. Each step has one measure (two at Augmentation and Agent-delegation), a threshold, and a minimum volume and period.
  2. Thresholds are global defaults. An organization may tighten them, never loosen them.
  3. Each step names its evidence method. Consequence of Error decides it: Low and Moderate may run a bounded live trial; High uses shadow or replay only; Critical never runs live at the next stage.
  4. High and Critical need double the volume, and a governance sign-off from Agent-delegation up. Automation always needs one.
  5. An audit has an independent auditor, a rubric drawn from the elements that change in the step, and discrepancies graded by severity: one serious miss is Not met, whatever the rate.
  6. The finding is Met, Not met or Insufficient Evidence. Below the minimum volume or period it is Insufficient Evidence; gray is never converted.
  7. Re-validation: on the review cadence, a measure back below its threshold flags the activity to step back.

Threshold numbers are starting points for Tim and GiGi to set, not established standards.
