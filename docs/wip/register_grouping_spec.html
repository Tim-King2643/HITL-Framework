Register Grouping by Framework Layer

Five groups that file every requirement by where it sits in the framework, with the current category kept as a topic tag
Documentation Dashboard Work in Progress
What this is

A new first-level grouping for the requirements register: Administration, Reference Model, Organizational Assessment, Assessment Output, and Human Impact & Readiness. It follows the framework’s own layers — the reference, assessing an organization against it, what the organization gets back — and gives GiGi’s side its own group, so the seam between the work side and the human-impact side shows in the register itself.

Decided
  • Sept 29: group requirements by framework layer (Tim), with a fifth group for the human-impact side, GiGi’s lead.
  • Sept 29: the current category stays on every requirement as a second-level topic tag; nothing is lost.
  • Sept 29: taken as a decision for both, since it files GiGi’s requirements under a group that defines her lead.
Awaiting your decision
If approved

Each requirement gets a group field and the register a group filter, applied as mapped in section 2 with any moves recorded in the decision notes. The category stays as the topic tag. Seven placements are marked ◆ as judgment calls (section 3).

Designs GR-057. Related: GR-051 make the register operational · GR-056 assessment output.

1. The groups

GroupWhat belongs here
AdministrationRunning, publishing and communicating the framework: website, language and precision, documentation, promotion, the register, the sandbox environment.
Reference ModelThe work-side model: the six categories and their definitions, Progression Ceilings and Consequence of Error, RACI and reporting, the change log and master data, the methodology.
Organizational AssessmentAssessing one organization against the reference: the assessment template, ceiling overrides, evidence rules.
Assessment OutputWhat the organization gets back and the role hand-off to the human-impact side (GR-056), including the per-role executive views.
Human Impact & ReadinessGiGi’s lead, from Human Impact onward: readiness, meaningful human control, judgment development, transition distance, the Human Impact Pathway.
