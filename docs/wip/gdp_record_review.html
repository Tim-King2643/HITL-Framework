Decision point records, for review

Every High and Critical control point needs a full Governed Decision Point record. 7.4.4 and 7.6.1 were approved on Nov 5. This page holds the other 32, written as proposals from each activity’s RACI, reporting line and Consequence of Error note, so you only need to react.

Each record has its own vote. Approve it as written, Not approve it with a note saying what to change, or Defer it. Once you have both approved a record, it is marked approved in the sandbox at the next push; a note on a Not approve becomes the revision.

The amber box on a record names the choices worth a closer look: an escalation outside the owner’s reporting line, a Governance Body* the organization must name, or a threshold the organization sets.
