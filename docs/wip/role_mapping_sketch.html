Their titles, everywhere our roles appear

The role catalog, RACI and records stay shared and keyed to the catalog roles. Each organization saves one more record: a role map from its own job titles to the catalog roles, with the match type and who confirmed it. When the organization is selected, every place a catalog role appears shows their title instead; hover any name for the catalog role behind it.

The titles here are made up for ABC Test Org to show every match type. The activities, RACI and decision point records are read live from the sandbox.

The display rules
  • One-to-one their title replaces ours.
  • Many-to-one all their titles are listed.
  • Split one title of theirs covers two catalog roles; it shows in both places, marked with the role it is acting as.
  • Unknown no title maps to a catalog role: shown in amber as “Unknown (role)”. On High or Critical work it holds the Readiness Gate until resolved.
  • Theirs only a title of theirs the catalog does not have: listed in the map, outside the model.
Recommended

Readiness stays recorded per catalog role, even when one title covers two: a person can be ready for one role’s work and not the other’s. The People view can combine a split title’s work under one panel, since that is how their people experience it.
