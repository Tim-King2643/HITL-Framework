Who holds the work here?

The organization walks its work in process and activity order. For every role the catalog’s RACI names on an activity (Accountable, Responsible, Consulted), it types the job title that actually holds that role here, or marks that nobody here does it. There is no list to pick from: the answers are theirs, and they are saved with the organization, so the walk shows them filled in when it is reopened.

Three things build from the answers: their title list (offered as they type, so later answers are a click), the role map from their titles to the catalog roles, and the different holders: activities where a role is held by someone other than usual, kept as that activity’s own RACI for the organization. The catalog itself stays shared and untouched.

How the role map reads the answers
  • A title is mapped to a catalog role when it holds that role in at least a quarter of the role’s answered activities.
  • One-to-one Many-to-one Split follow from the answers; Theirs only is a title of theirs never given a catalog role.
  • Unknown a catalog role answered “nobody here” everywhere. On High or Critical work it holds the Readiness Gate until resolved.
  • Different holder a role held by someone other than its usual title, once the role has at least three answers.
