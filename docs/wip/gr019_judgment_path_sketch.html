The judgment-building path

Agreed Nov 5 (GR-019): when AI takes the work people learned on, the learning has to be replaced on purpose. The trigger list computes which roles need a path; this sketch shows what an organization records for each one, and where the sandbox reads it.

What the organization records, per role
  • The path: what replaces the learning the role used to get from the work now moving to AI (the acceptance criterion: each such role names its path).
  • The judgment steps: how many people are at each of GiGi’s six steps, from Observe to Coach others.
  • Judgment Certificates: one per person moving to Decide independently, signed by the Accountable role on the decision it keeps, or by the role it reports to where the role is itself Accountable.
Not a gate

The path is individual development, read by the Readiness Gate as evidence on Capability / Skill (GR-012) for the decisions the role keeps. A role with no path named is evidence for a Conditional finding; the assessor still makes the rating, and nothing holds a step on its own. Findings lists the roles that need a path and have none named.

Recommended

The path is required for Operational roles and listed for review for the rest, as on the trigger list. Store it with the organization like its other assessments, one record per role; GiGi’s Recruiter pilot is the first one recorded.
