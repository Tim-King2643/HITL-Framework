The element grid from the human side

Six activities where the human questions bite hardest. Each card shows what the element grid says at the activity’s Progression Ceiling, the furthest AI may take it, and reads that against your five questions: the judgment that remains, accountability, decision authority, escalation burden, and whether the work left with the person gets lighter or harder. You record what it means for the person.

Everything here is Modeled, from the Reference Model. Nothing is about a real organization, so a finding is about the model or the instrument, or names the evidence an organization would need.

Set up once
  1. Open the sandbox. The Reference Model is enough; no assessment is needed.
  2. In Work, choose the case’s process group and open its process with +.
For each case
  1. In Work, click the activity’s steps up to its ceiling to see who does each part of the work at each stage, and its Decision Point for who answers for it.
  2. In People, “What is left for a person as the work moves up”, set the stage to the ceiling to see the role’s load.
  3. Write what it means for the person under each question; leave any blank that says nothing new.
  4. Say whether the model reads the case right, and what kind of finding it is.
Kinds of finding
  • Reference model: the grid, a ceiling or a role assignment looks wrong.
  • Instrument design: GR-012 needs to ask something differently.
  • Needs organization evidence: it can only be answered in a real organization.
