1Executive Summary

GR-012 answers a specific question that the work-side architecture cannot answer by itself: can the people and organization actually operate the proposed human-AI relationship? It is not a generic AI-readiness survey, a sentiment measure, or a technical validation instrument.

Can the organization operate this proposed human-AI relationship with real authority, capability, evidence, capacity, and escalation?

v0.2 keeps the ten-dimension instrument, makes its layers explicit, strengthens the evidence rules, keeps Gray/Insufficient Evidence legitimate, and preserves the decision not to collapse readiness into one composite score. It also incorporates the current work-side proposals on Human Intensity, weakest-link behavior, the top-of-catalog governance variant, and Human Criticality — with the refinements documented below.

2Purpose and Unit of Assessment

GR-012 must be applied to a specific proposed transition in the Working Relationship. It should never ask a broad question such as “Is HR ready for AI?” The unit is one activity/transition, with the work-side facts handed into Human Impact & Readiness.

Example assessment unit

Activity: Manage employee grievances. Current relationship: assessed state. Proposed relationship: the next intended working relationship, never beyond the Progression Ceiling. Readiness question: what must be true of the affected role(s) and organization for that specific transition to operate responsibly in practice?

3The Ten Readiness Dimensions — Same Instrument, Clearer Architecture

v0.2 keeps all ten dimensions. The change is architectural: users should be able to see whether a question tests a role/human operating condition, an organizational operating condition, or the shared evidence boundary.

Shared evidence condition — Trust / Evidence Quality. Technical evidence comes from the technical/process lane. GR-012 does not re-run technical validation; it tests whether the human side can see enough of the evidence, understand its limits and failure modes, and rely on it appropriately.

4Dimension Definitions and Evidence Questions

DimensionReadiness questionEvidence to look for
Role ClarityDoes the person understand what AI is allowed to do, what stays human, what must be reviewed/decided, and what cannot be delegated?Workflow, role language, RACI, training, scenario walkthrough.
Decision AuthorityWhen the person believes the AI-supported result is wrong, what are they actually allowed to do?Override rights, system permissions, escalation process, approval matrix, governed decision points.
Capability / SkillDoes the person have enough domain expertise and judgment to evaluate the AI-supported work independently?Case review, scenario testing, qualifications, exception-handling evidence.
Confidence / Decision FluencyIf the person and system disagree, will the person make and defend an independent decision?Scenario exercises, observed override behavior, manager feedback.
Psychological SafetyCan the person challenge the system and act on that concern without unreasonable personal or professional risk?Speak-up history, leadership behavior, escalation norms, incident reviews.
Capacity / WorkloadDoes the new relationship leave enough real capacity for meaningful review, exception handling and escalation?Caseload, review time, exception volume, backlog, staffing, verification effort.
Manager / Governance-Oversight ReadinessIs the surrounding manager or governance body prepared to coach, challenge, absorb escalation and govern the redesigned relationship?Manager/governance expectations, escalation criteria, monitoring, coaching, evidence of the actual oversight body.
Leadership Support / GovernanceWho owns the outcome and what happens when the new relationship fails?Decision rights, risk tolerance, stop conditions, escalation routes, named owners, governance standards.
Trust / Evidence QualityWhat evidence justifies asking the person to rely on the system at this level?Traceable technical evidence: type, source, reference, confidence, limitations, failure modes, real-volume results.
Change CapacityEven if the transition is right, can the organization absorb it now?Competing initiatives, staffing changes, implementation timing, leadership capacity, change load.

5Finding States and Evidence Rule

FindingMeaningEvidence rule
READYThe condition is in place and supported by evidence.Evidence demonstrates the operating condition exists in practice.
CONDITIONALThe transition may proceed only if a named condition, gap or control is resolved.Requires actual evidence of a gap or condition; a modeled concern alone is not enough.
NOT READYA material condition prevents responsible progression right now.Evidence shows a real stop condition or unacceptable operating gap.
INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCEThere is not enough organization-specific evidence to make a defensible readiness conclusion.Unknown stays unknown. This is not failure and is not “Not Ready.”
Non-negotiable evidence rule. Gray / Insufficient Evidence may not be converted into Ready, Conditional or Not Ready based only on reference-model assumptions, modeled concerns, or intuition. A finding changes only when organization-specific evidence supports the change.

7Candidate Hard Gates — Do Not Lock Yet

The first draft identified six dimensions that may behave like hard gates. v0.2 keeps them as a hypothesis until the same instrument is run across HRBP Manager, Recruiter and CHRO.

  • Decision Authority
  • Capability / Skill
  • Psychological Safety
  • Capacity / Workload
  • Leadership Support / Governance
  • Trust / Evidence Quality

Gate test: Did failure of this condition actually make the proposed human-AI relationship unsafe, non-credible, or impossible to operate responsibly? If the answer is not consistently yes across relevant pilots, the dimension should remain diagnostic/conditional rather than universal hard gate.

8v0.2 Decisions on the Four Work-Side Proposals

8.1 Human Intensity — qualitative finding, not a proxy score

Human Intensity remains a qualitative Human Impact & Readiness finding in v0.2. The work-side High/Critical share of work remaining with the person is useful evidence, but it is not the definition of intensity and should not be treated as a proxy for cognitive, emotional, relational or operational burden.

Human Intensity question

Does AI truly reduce human burden, or does it remove routine/easier work and concentrate the hardest cognitive, emotional, relational or operational work in the human?

8.2 Weakest-link behavior — material operational roles only

For High or Critical activities, readiness should roll up no stronger than the weakest materially required operational A/R role. Multiple roles should not be averaged into a reassuring composite. Low/Moderate activities can retain role-specific findings without a mandatory roll-up.

For a single decision-maker, the manager, executive committee or board should not automatically become another role in the same weakest-link calculation. That relationship is assessed separately as Manager / Governance-Oversight Readiness unless the body itself has an operational RACI responsibility for the activity.

8.3 Manager Absorption — top-of-catalog governance variant

Where a role has no manager above it in the role catalog, the framework should not display “no data.” Use a named Governance/Oversight Body variant. The actual body — board, executive committee or other governance structure — must be evidenced for the organization rather than assumed.

8.4 Human Criticality — interpret existing Consequence of Error evidence

Do not create a competing Human Criticality score. Use the existing Consequence of Error evidence — particularly Human Impact, Reach and Reversibility — as the work-side evidence base, then interpret what that consequence means for the human retaining the decision/control element.

Human Criticality should also be able to surface downstream human consequences even when the primary Consequence of Error factor is legal, operational or trust-related.

9GR-013 Human Agency / Meaningful Human Control

Definition

Human Agency / Meaningful Human Control is whether a person can actually exercise the judgment and accountability the operating model assigns to them, including the practical ability to understand, question, challenge, override, pause and escalate AI-supported work when appropriate.

Human Agency remains a distinct construct because “human present” is not the same as “human in meaningful control.” However, it should not become an eleventh GR-012 dimension or another readiness score. It should be a derived human-impact finding informed especially by Decision Authority, Capability/Skill, Confidence/Decision Fluency, Psychological Safety, Capacity/Workload, and usable Evidence Quality.

For executive/top-of-catalog roles, the agency question may shift. The issue may be less “can I override AI?” and more “do I have sufficiently trustworthy evidence, independent judgment and governance challenge to decide well?”

10Narrative Conclusion — No Composite Readiness Percentage

Each completed GR-012 assessment should end with a short narrative conclusion rather than a 0–100 score. The conclusion must show the outcome, the evidence behind it, the principal gaps, and what must happen before progression.

Narrative template

[Role / activity] is [Ready / Conditional / Not Ready / Insufficient Evidence] for the proposed transition. The strongest operating conditions are ____. The principal unresolved conditions are ____. The available evidence shows ____. Before progression, the organization must ____. Human Agency is [supported / constrained / not yet evidenced] because ____.

11Pilot Sequence and Pressure-Test Plan

The same ten dimensions must be used without adding, dropping or redefining a dimension between pilots. The success condition is not identical findings across roles. The success condition is a stable method that produces credible, role-specific findings.

11.1 Pilot 1 — HRBP Manager / Manage employee grievances

Status: first pass complete. The instrument correctly produced Insufficient Evidence where organization-specific proof was missing. The pilot should remain the baseline example for Gray/unknown discipline and for separating organization findings from instrument-design findings.

11.2 Pilot 2 — Recruiter / 7.2.3.4 Select and reject candidates

Purpose: pressure-test the same instrument on Critical, higher-volume operational work and test weakest-link behavior without changing the ten dimensions.

DimensionRecruiter pressure-test question
Role ClarityDoes Recruiter know exactly what AI may do in sourcing/screening/ranking versus what remains the human select/reject decision?
Decision AuthorityCan Recruiter reject, override, pause or challenge an AI ranking/recommendation?
Capability / SkillCan Recruiter detect biased, incomplete, weak or misleading AI-supported candidate recommendations?
Confidence / Decision FluencyWill Recruiter challenge a confident-looking AI recommendation when independent judgment differs?
Psychological SafetyCan Recruiter slow, stop or challenge the process without pressure to accept AI output for speed or volume?
Capacity / WorkloadIs there enough real time to review the harder cases that remain after AI absorbs more routine work?
Manager / Governance-Oversight ReadinessIs Director of TA prepared to support, govern and absorb escalations under the new operating model?
Leadership Support / GovernanceAre hiring decision rights, escalation rules, controls and accountability operationally defined?
Trust / Evidence QualityCan Recruiter see enough traceable evidence, limitations and failure modes to rely appropriately on AI-supported screening/ranking?
Change CapacityCan recruiting absorb the new workflow, capability requirements and escalation model at real operating volume?

Pilot rule: Where organization-specific evidence is unavailable, record Insufficient Evidence and explicitly state what would move the finding. Do not infer readiness from the reference model.

11.3 Pilot 3 — CHRO / 7.1.1.2 Define HR and business function roles and accountability

Purpose: test whether the same instrument holds at executive level, where authority, governance and organizational consequence are shaped differently and there is no traditional manager-above relationship inside the role catalog.

  • Use the same ten dimensions with no redefinition.
  • Use the Governance/Oversight Body variant instead of treating top-of-catalog as “no data.”
  • Test whether the relevant governance body is evidenced rather than assumed.
  • Pay special attention to Decision Authority, Leadership/Governance, Evidence Quality, Change Capacity and the executive form of Human Agency.
  • End with the same narrative conclusion format used for HRBP Manager and Recruiter.

12Decisions to Make Only After the Three Pilots

DecisionQuestion the pilots must answer
Hard gatesWhich dimensions repeatedly behaved as true stop conditions rather than remediable diagnostics?
Weakest-link ruleDid the rule protect High/Critical work without over-penalizing roles that were not materially required?
Executive exceptionDid the Governance/Oversight Body variant work for CHRO and other top-of-catalog cases?
Human AgencyDid the derived GR-013 finding add information beyond the ten dimensions without duplicating them?
Human IntensityDid qualitative findings reveal burden that the High/Critical share could not explain on its own?
Evidence schemaWere evidence type, source role, reference, confidence, date and coverage sufficient to move findings defensibly?
Instrument stabilityDid the same ten dimensions hold without redefinition across Managerial, Operational and Executive roles?

13Design Guardrails

  • Do not turn the ten dimensions into one readiness percentage.
  • Do not treat Insufficient Evidence as failure or as Not Ready.
  • Do not turn Modeled potential into an observed readiness finding.
  • Do not re-run technical validation inside GR-012; consume technical evidence with provenance.
  • Do not average materially different role-level findings into one reassuring number.
  • Do not treat a governance body as an operational touching role unless it actually holds operational responsibility.
  • Do not use High/Critical work share as the definition of Human Intensity.
  • Do not create a second Human Criticality rating that can disagree with Consequence of Error.
  • Do not automate scoring or polish the dashboard until the manual evidence logic survives all three pilots.
