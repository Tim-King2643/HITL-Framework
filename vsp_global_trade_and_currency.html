Description

This value stream comprises two APQC PCF process groups: 9.10 Manage International Funds/Consolidation and 9.11 Perform Global Trade Services — spanning 14 Level 3 processes, the most of any PCF 9.0 value stream, though neither group decomposes into Level 4 activities in the underlying APQC taxonomy, so each L3 stands as its own single activity unit.

At the activity level, this value stream's work breaks down as 7 Transaction activities, 4 Document activities, 2 Knowledge activities, and 1 Exception activity.

At M3 (HITL Design Intent), the value stream's aggregate human-involvement ratio is 44%, down from a 70% undesigned baseline — a 26-point AI convergence opportunity, the steepest in PCF 9.0.

Design Narrative

Global Trade & Currency spans two APQC process groups — 9.10 Manage International Funds/Consolidation and 9.11 Perform Global Trade Services — and is PCF 9.0's most fragmented value stream by accountability: no single role holds a majority of its 14 L3s. VP Treasury carries the largest single share at 4 of 14 (international rate monitoring, transaction management, currency-exposure hedging, and letter-of-credit preparation), but Trade Compliance — an external, no-catalog role rather than one of the domain's 14 named roles — is Accountable or Responsible across nearly the entire 9.11 group, the same kind of specialist-role gap PCF 7.0 documented for International HR Management in Global Mobility. This value stream classifies Enablement rather than Operational or Strategy because trade compliance, customs, and currency-risk work is cross-cutting protective infrastructure supporting every other stream's cross-border transactions, not a value chain in its own right — the same reasoning that places PCF 8.0's Risk-to-Resilience in Enablement despite Executive-tier accountable ownership. Its steepest AI-absorption opportunity sits in 9.10.4 (Report results, Document-pattern, -32 points).

Handoff Points

Global Trade & Currency runs two largely parallel lanes rather than a single linear flow: VP Treasury's international funds/currency lane (9.10) and Trade Compliance's customs/export-control lane (9.11).

VP Treasury's lane opens with rate monitoring and transaction management (9.10.1, 9.10.2), runs through currency-exposure hedging (9.10.3), and closes with consolidated results reporting (9.10.4) — feeding directly into Record-to-Report's consolidated financial statements.

Trade Compliance's lane runs sanctioned-party screening through customs documentation and duty calculation (9.11.1–9.11.8), with Internal Audit Manager and Tax Manager stepping in only for restitution handling (9.11.9) — the lane's one Exception-pattern activity — and for duty/trade-preference calculation, the two points where the external Trade Compliance role hands off to a named Finance role.
