Description

This value stream comprises two APQC PCF process groups: 9.3 Perform General Accounting and Reporting and 9.8 Manage Internal Controls — spanning 7 Level 3 processes and 42 Level 4 activities, the largest value stream in PCF 9.0 by both measures.

At the activity level, this value stream's work breaks down as 20 Transaction activities, 12 Document activities, 5 Decision activities, 4 Knowledge activities, and 1 Exception activity.

At M3 (HITL Design Intent), the value stream's aggregate human-involvement ratio is 49%, down from a 73% undesigned baseline — a 24-point AI convergence opportunity.

Design Narrative

Record-to-Report spans two APQC process groups — 9.3 General Accounting and Reporting and 9.8 Manage Internal Controls — and its accountability pattern reflects that combined scope: VP Controller holds a plurality rather than a majority, Accountable for 3 of 7 L3s, but those three are the stream's framing L3s — financial-policy setting (9.3.1), financial reporting itself (9.3.4), and internal-controls establishment (9.8.1) — the stream's policy-in/report-out boundary. Director of Accounting owns the transactional core between those bookends: general accounting (9.3.2) and fixed-asset accounting (9.3.3). Internal Audit Manager owns the stream's compliance-monitoring half (9.8.2, 9.8.3), operating and reporting on the controls VP Controller establishes. This is the largest value stream in PCF 9.0 by both L3 count (7) and activity count (42), and industry finance-transformation frameworks — including The Hackett Group's global business services benchmarking — treat record-to-report as one of finance's three core operational value chains alongside order-to-cash and procure-to-pay, not a supporting function, which is why it classifies Operational rather than Enablement despite carrying the domain's internal-controls governance. Its steepest AI-absorption opportunity sits in 9.3.1.3 (Publish accounting policies, Document-pattern, -32 points).

Handoff Points

Record-to-Report opens with VP Controller-accountable policy setting (9.3.1), then hands to Director of Accounting for the transactional core — general accounting and fixed-asset accounting (9.3.2, 9.3.3).

Director of Accounting's postings and reconciliations feed VP Controller's financial reporting (9.3.4) — business-unit statements, consolidation, and board/regulatory filings.

In parallel, Internal Audit Manager operates and reports on the internal controls VP Controller establishes (9.8.1) — monitoring control effectiveness, remediating deficiencies, and reporting compliance status (9.8.2, 9.8.3) back to VP Controller and the CFO.
